Data notes

Quality checks performed against the supplied CSV.
  • State-total mismatch: CSV sum = 73,975,672 versus official 2011 Tamil Nadu population = 72,147,030. Difference = 1,828,642 (+2.53%).
  • District geography: the CSV contains 38 current districts, whereas the 2011 Census district geography had 32 districts. Several current districts were created after 2011 and therefore require boundary reconstruction if presented as "2011 Census by today's districts."
  • Chennai: CSV population/area implies 14,596.6/km², while the CSV says 10,052/km². Official Tamil Nadu government 2011 figures are 4,646,732 people and 175 km² for Chennai.
  • Most other density rows: listed density is close to population ÷ area, generally within normal rounding/area-definition differences. Salem is a notable smaller discrepancy of about 5 people/km².
  • Business interpretation: population and density are market-sizing proxies, not direct measures of parcel demand, railway connectivity, traveller availability, willingness to use Vahananjal, or last-mile cost.
Official reference: Tamil Nadu Statistical Handbook, 2011 Census district population tables; Census of India Population Finder / Primary Census Abstract.
